# SchnapShot — 10-second promo director script

## Intent

Make SchnapShot feel like the shortest path between “I can see it” and “the site accepts it.” The film should be precise, fast, and Mac-native—not magical for magic’s sake. Orange is the active signal. Charcoal is the workspace. The real interface is the proof.

## Format

- Runtime: exactly 10 seconds
- Master: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC
- Structure: two generated 5-second shots, assembled locally
- Sound: restrained UI impacts followed by a warm three-note resolve
- Readability rule: no generated copy; supplied typography and UI are locked

## Beat sheet

### 0:00–0:02.2 — Identify the product

**Picture:** Begin on the SchnapShot icon and name. The application result enters with a controlled right-to-left camera glide.

**On-screen message:** “Your screenshot. Site-ready.”

**Direction:** Establish confidence immediately. No decorative effects; the UI itself is the hero.

### 0:02.2–0:05.0 — Show the requirement

**Picture:** Continue into the preset selection, resolving clearly on `1200 × 630` and the saved Thumbnail card.

**On-screen message:** “The size your site needs.”

**Direction:** Let the exact dimensions become the visual punchline. Hold long enough to read.

### 0:05.0–0:07.8 — Reveal the engine and result

**Picture:** Move from the generated thumbnail result toward the copied-to-clipboard confirmation.

**On-screen message:** “Your Codex. Your image gen.”

**Direction:** Keep the claim literal: generation uses OpenAI through the user’s Codex account; local capture and output stay in the Mac workflow.

### 0:07.8–0:10.0 — Brand resolve

**Picture:** Pull back into the centered SchnapShot icon and wordmark. Finish cleanly with a short visual hold.

**On-screen message:** “Capture. Generate. Copy.”

**Direction:** The final chord lands with the icon. End on certainty, not spectacle.

## Negative direction

- Do not add people, hands, phones, laptops, or invented environments.
- Do not invent or rewrite product UI.
- Do not claim generation is offline.
- Do not expose sign-in codes or credentials.
- Do not introduce unsupported download, pricing, or availability claims.

## Revision two — transition treatment

The first cut asked the model to transform between layouts, which caused an unstable morph near 3 seconds, followed by an abrupt edit at 5 seconds. Revision two separates generation from editorial transitions:

- Filmflow generates two steady motion plates from identical start and end frames.
- The preset and closing compositions remain exact supplied stills.
- A 0.2-second editorial dip-through-black bridges each layout at 3.0, 5.0, and 8.0 seconds without stacking the typography.
- The resulting rhythm is approximately 3 seconds of product setup, 2 seconds of preset proof, 3 seconds of Codex proof, and 2 seconds of brand resolve.
