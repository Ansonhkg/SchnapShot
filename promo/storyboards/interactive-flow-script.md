# SchnapShot — interactive 10-second promo

## Direction

Approved logo: `../assets/schnapshot-approved-shot-glass-logo.png`. Use this exact user-supplied artwork for all new storyboard branding and the video ending. Preserve the shot-glass silhouette, amber liquid, screenshot motif, and orange spark. Composite the actual asset rather than asking image or video generation to redraw it. The original promo supplies the ending's composition, transition, and sound only; replace its old icon with this approved logo.

The viewer should feel a real person moving through the workflow. Every transition is motivated by an action: pan, hover, click, drag, render, copy, replace. Avoid passive slides and layout morphs. Keep the cursor visible whenever it causes the next shot.

## Motion rules

- Use cursor movement to establish where the eye should go before every click.
- Show a visible hover state before a click and a ring or compression on contact.
- Use pans and zooms to connect spatially related content, not decorative camera drift.
- Keep all UI and text rigid; never ask video generation to morph one interface into another.
- Handle browser opening, shutter, replacement, and ending as controlled editorial transitions.
- Master at exactly 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, and 10 seconds.
